Mockarr drives a fake route through the real world using Android's built-in "mock location" developer feature — the OS-sanctioned way to test location-aware apps. Pick points on a map, get a realistic road route, press Play: your device's reported location drives that route with human-like acceleration, corner slow-downs, wait times at stops and GPS-style noise, visible in any app that reads location.

Features:

• Tap or search to place stops; a real road route is generated between them
• Playback with human-like acceleration, braking before turns and GPS-style noise
• Wait times at stops — the drive pulls up, waits, and moves on
• Pin mode: long-press to hold your location anywhere, with a thumbstick to nudge it
• Background playback with Pause / Resume / Stop from the notification; survives screen-off
• Save routes and replay them fully offline
• Live speed control (0.25× – 4×)
• No accounts, no sign-in

Setup takes a minute: enable Developer Options, then choose Mockarr under "Select mock location app". The in-app checklist walks you through each step and shows what is still missing.

Mockarr is an honest testing tool. It uses only the official Android mock-location API — nothing is patched, hooked or rooted — and it contains no features that hide mock status from other apps. Mock locations exist for testing; how you use them in third-party services is your responsibility, and some services' terms prohibit location spoofing.
