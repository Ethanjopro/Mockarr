## Platform Conformance Verdict
**Pass: this reads as a native Material 3 app.**
- Predictive back is opted in (`enableOnBackInvokedCallback`), and both `BackHandler`s are
  conditional.
- `enableEdgeToEdge()`, with safe-drawing insets applied at the root.
- One icon family (`res/drawable/ic_*`, no `material-icons`).
- M3 dialogs, sheets, chips, switches and snackbars.

The deliberate deviations are documented in DESIGN.md: the Strava-style action row (three
large circles instead of a FAB + bottom app bar), and pills instead of text buttons. A
fluent Android user trips on neither.

## Fixed this round
- **[P1] Stop markers had a ~27dp touch target** (`map/MarkerHitTest.kt`). A thumb missed
  them, most on a moving map. A tap within 24dp of a stop's centre now selects it (48dp);
  a tap on a drawn disc still resolves overlaps first. Tests cover the reach and the overlap
  rule. Verified: a tap 19dp from centre, outside the disc, selects the stop.
- **[P2] The dark sheet's top edge met the map at 1.07:1** (`MapScreen.kt` sheet column).
  The sheet now wears the floating family's 1dp rim in dark: 3.71:1, measured.
- **[P2] The Saved routes search field had no border at rest**: 1.04:1 dark, 1.05:1 light
  against the page (WCAG 1.4.11). It now uses the M3 `outline` role: 5.86:1 dark, 4.27:1
  light.
- **[P2] The map search bar spanned the whole screen on tablets** (`MapScreen.kt`). It is now
  capped at `sheetMaxWidth` and centred, like the card and the sheet.
- **[P2] State shown only visually**, now exposed to TalkBack:
  - the Saved routes sort order: check icon only → items are exposed as checked
  - the Follow map pill's filled state: colour only → `selected`
  - the builder's selected stop row: tint only → `selected`
- **[P3] Missing roles:** the builder's ✕ and the Settings tiles are now announced as buttons.
- **[P3] Literal marker-shadow colour in map code** (`SearchPin.kt`, `WaypointMarkers.kt`)
  moved into `MapPalette.markerShadow` (CLAUDE.md: map colours come from `MapPalette`).
- DESIGN.md: its "side panel planned, not yet built" line was stale. It now documents the
  panel, the search bar cap, the 48dp stop target and the sheet rim.

## Remaining findings
- **[P2] The search fields have a placeholder but no label** (`MapSearchComponents.kt:79`,
  `SavedRoutesScreen.kt:119`). TalkBack reads the placeholder only while the field is empty;
  once filled, the field is unnamed. The fix is either a visible label or a semantics-only
  name. That's a visual call → **loose end 4 (Ethan's design batch)**.
- **[P2] Stops can be placed without gestures only via search, and Move stop needs a
  drag** (TalkBack / switch access). The sheet row "Hold my location at the map centre"
  covers holds but not stops → **loose end 4** (already on its list).
- **[P3] 3D map and units toggles** say their state through a changing description ("Switch
  to 3D map", "Units, Miles") rather than toggle semantics. Workable, not idiomatic →
  `/impeccable polish`.
- **[P3] Setup's inline links** are only as tall as a line of body text (`SetupScreen.kt`) →
  `/impeccable polish`.
- **[P3] Marker and chip text scales only to 1.3×** (bitmaps, `MockarrMap.kt:173`). This is
  deliberate: larger markers occlude the route.
- **[P3] MapLibre's own fling and double-tap animations** ignore Remove animations. The
  app's own camera moves honour it.
- **[P3] Launch time was not measured**, and the new `play-services-location` adds weight
  (ADR 0005) → a later `/impeccable optimize` pass.

## Patterns
- The recurring gap is **state shown only by colour or tint** (sort check, Follow, the
  selected stop). All three found are fixed. New selectable controls should set
  `selected` / `toggleableState` from day one.
- **Contrast against the map in dark** is the other repeat (floating items in session 41,
  the sheet now). Every dark surface that meets the map needs the rim.
